# Privacy Release & Recording Consent

Please read and sign before running `./start.sh`.

## What gets recorded

During this session we capture:

- **A terminal transcript** (`session.log`), produced by `./start.sh`.
- **An optional agent event log** (`interview.log`) — your prompts to your AI
  tool and the names of the tools it invoked, if your tool supports project
  hooks and you have them enabled.
- **The repository you push at the end**, including its git history.
- **Screen sharing** for the duration of the live session, and — only if you
  agree below — a screen recording of it.
- **Written notes** taken by the proctor.

## What does not get recorded

- Anything outside this repository and the shared screen.
- Any window or application you do not share.
- Your personal accounts, credentials, or files. If your AI tool would
  transmit something you consider private, don't run it.

## How it's used

- Solely to evaluate your candidacy for this role.
- Read by the hiring team at Stratus. Not shared outside the company, not
  published, not used as training data, and not used as marketing or teaching
  material.
- Retained for up to **12 months** from the session date, then deleted. You may
  request deletion sooner by emailing the recruiter on this loop.

## Your choices

- You may decline the screen recording and still do the interview. The live
  session and the terminal transcript are required; the recording is not.
- You may ask the proctor to pause recording at any point.
- You may withdraw from the session at any point.
